few fact about the world we live in

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only known object to harbor life. This is possible because it is an ocean world, with liquid water covering 70.8% of its crust, while the remaining 29.2% is dry land. Its dynamic tectonic plates constantly move to form mountains and volcanoes, while its liquid outer core generates a protective magnetic field that shields the planet from destructive solar winds and radiation.
